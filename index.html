Quit / switch / hire gates
When to switch jobs

After 3 polished cases + 2 paid outcomes + workflow proof, run a 30-application experiment. Switch only for materially higher pay and better creative/technical optionality; planning target ₹6L+ annual, not a guarantee.

When to quit

All must pass: 6 months operating profit ≥2× essential expenses and ≥1.5× current take-home; 6–9 months personal runway; credible 3-month pipeline; no client above 35%; compliance and payment route working.

When to hire

Backlog over 2 weeks for 6 weeks, founder utilization over 70%, repeatable SOP, and contractor leaves at least 55–60% gross margin. Start per project.

When to build a product

The same paid pain appears in 8+ interviews, 3 paid design partners or 5 real deposits exist, MVP is ≤12 weeks, variable cost is <20%, and one acquisition channel is reachable.

11 · Decision tree

Choose based on evidence

This short tree tells you whether to keep the service path, emphasize automation, use the portfolio for a job switch, or build a validated product.

30-day gate

Message evidence

≥5% positive replies: continue. Under 5% after 100 relevant contacts: change trigger, niche or message—not your logo.

60-day gate

Sales evidence

Replies but fewer than 3 calls/100: change CTA. No close after 8 qualified calls: revise proof, risk, scope or price.

90-day gate

Market evidence

No paid work after 300 contacts across 2 niches: stop treating the service as primary; use proof for a role switch and test automation.

13 · Risk dashboard

Most failures are predictable

Production is not the hardest problem. Distribution, trust, rights, differentiation and sustainable capacity are.

HIGH · Acquisition
Risk: portfolio work creates no clients.
Mitigation: 25 qualified accounts/week, trigger-led outreach, agency partnerships, 100-contact diagnostic gates.
HIGH · Commoditization
Risk: AI compresses simple-video prices.
Mitigation: sell product understanding, concepts, channel adaptation, rights-safe QA, versioning and a learning system.
HIGH · Overwork
Risk: full-time job + bespoke delivery becomes unsustainable.
Mitigation: 11.5-hour schedule, approval freeze, scope caps, one revision, no more than two parallel sprints.
MEDIUM · Rights
Risk: unlicensed assets, voices, likenesses or misleading synthetic content.
Mitigation: provenance log, written consent, commercial licences, platform disclosures, client warranties and human QA.
MEDIUM · Payment/compliance
Risk: failed collection, missing export records or wrong tax assumptions.
Mitigation: Wise if eligible, Payoneer/direct fallback, Zoho invoices, records and an India-qualified CA.
MEDIUM · Client concentration
Risk: one client becomes the business.
Mitigation: no client >35% before quitting, keep outbound active and retain reusable pre-existing tools/SOPs.
MEDIUM · Premature software
Risk: spend a year building a tool nobody buys.
Mitigation: 8 repeated-pain interviews + 3 paid partners/5 deposits + ≤12-week MVP gate.

Original IP rule

Build “Bug Gremlin,” not a cinematic universe

Create a reusable original character system as a low-cost comedy R&D lab and client proof. Cap at 2 hours/week and 10% of realized business profit. After 8 shorts, continue only if production gets faster, an owned audience grows, or commercial enquiries appear. Views alone are not the gate.

15 · Evidence discipline

Facts are not forecasts

Every major recommendation separates observable market evidence from planning inputs and unknowable outcomes.

  • US digital advertising revenue approached $300B in 2025, up 13.9% year over year (IAB/PwC, published April 2026).
  • Current platform and job evidence shows demand for AI creative production, motion, product visualization and creative automation.
  • YunoJuno’s mature-market 2026 report lists directional averages of $86/hour creative technologist, $74 AI automation and $64 motion graphics—not beginner entitlements.
  • Stripe India remains invite-only; Wise/Payoneer/direct transfer are alternative collection routes subject to eligibility.
  • TikTok remains blocked/unavailable in India; it is not part of this operating plan.
  • Offer prices, India/US/UK/EU ranges, close rates, time-to-income, costs, and success probabilities are estimates—not guarantees.
  • ₹50K/₹1L/₹2L/₹5L probabilities are conditional judgment ranges, not measured success rates.
  • Contractor, software, infrastructure and support costs in projections are modeled estimates.
  • Planning FX is ₹90/US$; actual settlement varies.
  • You can invest 10–13 focused hours/week for 48 weeks without violating employment terms.
  • You complete three strong proof projects by day 45 and send 500 relevant contacts over 20+ weeks.
  • Targets mean gross business revenue sustained for 3 months; tax-reserve percentages are cash-planning inputs, not liabilities.
  • No representative dataset gives a new India-based part-time creative freelancer’s probability of success.
  • Creative quality, sales skill, client trust, economic conditions, platform rules, FX and luck can change outcomes.
  • AI commercial-use terms do not guarantee copyrightability, uniqueness or non-infringement.
  • India GST/export/income-tax treatment depends on facts; verify with a qualified professional.

The direct answer

If I were starting from your exact position, this is what I would do.

I would keep the TCS role and spend the next 30 days learning only enough Figma, DaVinci Resolve/Fusion, motion fundamentals, product storytelling and ethical AI-assisted production to create three commercial proof pieces. I would not disappear into courses, learn every design discipline, build a game, buy a camera, or become an educator.

I would build a faceless studio brand around the work—not myself—and sell one precise outcome to B2B SaaS/AI firms: a $650 feature-launch loop, then a $1,250 product-story sprint. I would target companies with a launch in the last 60 days and contact 25 each week with a specific visual gap and, for the best five, a six-second concept.

I would use AI for concept breadth, storyboards, cleanup, voice and variants, but keep human judgment, rights, consistency and product truth as the value. After paid work, I would map repeated approval/versioning/localization pain and sell a workflow automation build. Only after 3 paid design partners or 5 deposits would I build a narrow plugin or micro-SaaS.

I would aim for ₹1L gross through one premium sprint per month, but not quit until six months of stable profit, 6–9 months runway, a three-month pipeline and low client concentration. I would attempt ₹5L through two retainers, sprints and contractor leverage—not five times my hours. Original comedy IP would get two hours per week until evidence earns more.

The 12-month thesis: employment for runway → service for cash and customer truth → automation for leverage → software/IP for ownership.
