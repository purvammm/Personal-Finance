**Top reasons and red flags**

- **Vishal Nirmiti**
  1. Only ~1% of the issue is reserved for QIBs, so there's no institutional signal (no QIB demand point possible) and no anchor book was reported.
  2. Demand is weak so far, and GMP fell 43% in a day.
  3. Revenue grew about 18% a year over FY24–26 and the EBITDA margin rose from 9.5% to 15.1% (+2 points), but FY26 growth was only ~6%. Anand Rathi calls it fully priced at 23.2x FY26 earnings.
  - *Unusual:* a ~70% retail / 29% NII structure means few institutions are underwriting the price.
- **Nityas Gems**
  1. It's a **Reg 6(2) issue**: no profit track record under Reg 6(1), so ≥75% must go to QIBs (and, per my reading of the rules, the issue fails if that isn't met). QIBs were at 0.38x.
  2. **Operating cash flow was −₹14.7 crore in FY26**, with 167 working-capital days, despite fast growth (revenue ₹54 → ₹203 crore in two years, +3 points).
  3. Concentration: the top-10 suppliers are 86% of purchases, there's a single Surat plant, and five states make up 85% of revenue.

**What would change the labels on Monday:** APPLY needs a score ≥14 *and* demand points ≥2. For Vishal, that means NII ≥10x and retail ≥3x, plus clean RHP checks. That's unlikely from today's starting point, but day 3 is when QIBs and HNIs bid. Re-score at about 2 PM.

## 4. Recent listings → market mood

- **30 Sep – 1 Oct:** Adroit **+75%**, Moneyview **+62%**, A-One **+12%** (closed +3%), Swastika **+8%**, ArMee **0%** (−15% intraday), Elevate **−1.9%**.
- **Mood: a two-speed market.**
  - Issues with ≥30x demand still pop; issues under 3x list flat or lower, and pops fade within hours.
  - This is happening while the **Nifty fell 6.1% in September** (8 straight weekly falls) and foreign investors sold about ₹36,000 crore.
  - 145 SEBI-approved IPOs (about ₹2.78 lakh crore) are waiting. Heavy supply plus a falling market is a headwind for listing gains.
