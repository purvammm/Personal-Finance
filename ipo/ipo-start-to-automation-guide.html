1 · The honest 2-minute version

What you're actually doing: applying for one lot (≈ ₹15,000) of a new company's shares before they list, then usually selling on listing day. You pay only if you're allotted shares. When an IPO is popular, allotment is a lottery, and your money is only blocked in your bank account for about 5 working days.

✅ You can expect

  • Allotment in a minority of the popular IPOs you bid on (often 1–15% odds each), and near-certain allotment in unpopular ones.
  • Gains of roughly ₹1,500–12,000 on an allotted lot in a hot IPO. Weak IPOs often end between −₹2,000 and +₹750.
  • Over a year, on a ₹15,000–45,000 pool: roughly −₹5,000 to +₹25,000 before tax, depending heavily on the market's mood (estimate, file 01).
  • A real education in prospectuses, valuation and how the market works. That's the main return.

❌ You cannot expect

  • Monthly income, or any guaranteed listing gain.
  • Better odds from bidding more lots. In the retail category, more lots don't raise your odds; they only block more money.
  • To fund the ≈ ₹10 lakh Canada proof of funds through IPOs. Your savings rate does that (section 22).
  • A bot that applies for you. You approve every UPI mandate yourself (section 25).
The maths of one good applicationA-One Steels (listed 1 Oct 2026): retail odds were at least 11%. An allotted lot sold at the open made about ₹1,850 before costs. Expected value per application: 11% × ₹1,850 ≈ ₹200. For comparison, ₹30,000 in a 6.5% FD earns about ₹1,950 a year with no effort. The scorecard's main job is to keep you out of the −15% days, not to find jackpots.

The whole strategy on one screen

  1. Emergency fund first. No live bids until you hold 3 months' expenses in cash (22). Paper-trade until then; it teaches the same lessons.
  2. Paper-trade for at least 2 weeks with the journal (20).
  3. Mainboard only, retail category, 1 lot, at the cut-off price. No SME issues: their minimum is two lots, about ₹2.4–2.9 lakh.
  4. Decide on the closing day after about 2 PM, when the institutional (QIB) and HNI (NII) numbers come in. Bid before 3 PM and approve the UPI mandate at once. The hard deadline is 5 PM.
  5. APPLY only if the scorecard shows no knock-outs, 14/20 or more, and at least 2 of the 3 closing-day demand points (14). You can lower a label, never raise it.
  6. Write your exit plan before listing day. Opens above issue → sell within the first 30 minutes. Opens below issue → exit that day. Never buy more.
  7. Stop rules: 3 losing allotments in a row → 30 days of paper only. A realised loss above ₹3,000 in a quarter → paper only until the quarter ends.
  8. One PAN, one bid per IPO, your own UPI ID. A second bid on the same PAN gets both rejected.
  9. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, TPIN or password with anyone: no "IPO group", no "support agent", no bot.
  10. Automate the research and the reminders, never the bid.

4 · How an IPO works (plain English)

Ten terms you'll use every week

TermPlain meaning
IPOA company sells shares to the public for the first time, then lists on NSE/BSE.
Mainboard / SMEMainboard is the normal exchange. SME (NSE Emerge, BSE SME) is for small companies: looser rules, a two-lot minimum (≈ ₹2.4–2.9 lakh), thin trading. Your rules exclude SME.
DRHP / RHPDraft / Red Herring Prospectus: the offer document. The RHP adds the price band and dates. The abridged prospectus is a short summary of it.
Price band, cut-offThe range you can bid in. Ticking cut-off means "I accept the final price". The amount blocked is at the top of the band.
LotThe minimum number of shares per bid, set so one lot costs ₹10,000–15,000 on the mainboard.
Fresh issue / OFSFresh issue: new shares, and the money goes to the company. Offer for sale (OFS): existing owners sell, and the money goes to them.
QIB / NII / RIIInstitutions / HNIs bidding above ₹2 lakh (small NII ₹2–10 lakh, big NII above ₹10 lakh) / retail individuals bidding up to ₹2 lakh. You are RII.
Anchor investorsBig institutions allotted shares the working day before the issue opens. Half their shares are locked in for 30 days and the rest for 90.
ASBA / UPI mandateApplication Supported by Blocked Amount. Your money stays in your bank account and is only blocked. A broker-app bid creates the block through a UPI mandate that you approve with your UPI PIN.
Subscription (×)Shares bid ÷ shares offered, per category. "20x retail" means retail bids were 20 times the retail quota.
T, T+1, T+2, T+3T = the closing day. T+1: allotment. T+2: shares credited and unused money unblocked. T+3: listing. These are working days, so weekends and NSE holidays are skipped.
GMPGrey market premium: an unofficial, unregulated price quote before listing. Noisy. It's worth at most 1 point on your scorecard.

The life of an IPO

Months before
The company files a DRHP with SEBI. SEBI's "observations" are effectively clearance.
~1 week before
The RHP is filed and the price band and dates announced. This is when your research starts.
Open day − 1
Anchor book. The names of the big institutions that took shares are published.
3 working days
Bidding window. Days 1–2 are mostly retail noise. Institutions and HNIs bid on the last afternoon, so you decide after ~2 PM on the closing day (T).
T (closing day)
Bid before 3 PM. Zerodha's window ends at 4:45 PM. Approve the UPI mandate by 5 PM.
T+1
Basis of allotment. Check the registrar's site in the evening.
T+2
Allotted: the amount is debited and shares reach your demat (shown as "NA" price until listing). Not allotted: the money is unblocked.
T+3 (listing)

5 · Allotment: the retail lottery

When retail is oversubscribed, the registrar works out how many minimum lots the retail quota contains (retail shares ÷ lot size), then draws lots. Each winner gets one lot. If applicants bid k lots on average, your odds are about k ÷ retail subscription, which is at least 1 ÷ retail subscription.

Adverse selectionBig, boring issues give high odds and small pops. Small, hot issues give big pops and terrible odds. You will mostly be allotted in the IPOs nobody else wanted. That's exactly why the scorecard has a demand gate.

Why an application can fail even when you "applied"

  • You didn't approve the UPI mandate by 5 PM on the closing day.
  • The UPI ID or bank account isn't in your name. A family member's UPI gets rejected.
  • Two bids on one PAN in the same category, even through different brokers: both are rejected.
  • The bank or UPI app dropped the mandate. Zerodha says to delete and re-submit if no mandate arrives within an hour.
  • A PAN or demat mismatch.
  • The issue itself failed: for example, a Reg 6(2) issue that couldn't fill its QIB quota refunds everyone.
Separate quotas are allowed, not extra lottery ticketsSome RHPs reserve a slice for employees, policyholders or the parent's shareholders. Jio Platforms' draft mentions a Reliance shareholder quota. Bidding in a quota the RHP explicitly allows you is legal; check the RHP's eligibility and record date. Don't buy a parent company's shares just to qualify unless you'd hold them anyway.

6 · Pre-flight checks ⏱ 20–40 min Free

Most failed account openings come down to one of three things: an inoperative PAN, an Aadhaar that isn't linked to your current mobile, or names that don't match. Fix these first, because some fixes take days.

  1. Is your PAN operative and linked to Aadhaar? On the income-tax e-filing portal → Link Aadhaar Status, enter PAN and Aadhaar. If it says not linked, complete the portal's "Link Aadhaar" flow first (a fee applies when you link late). An inoperative PAN stalls KYC.
  2. Is Aadhaar linked to the mobile in your hand? Check on UIDAI's myAadhaar ("Verify Email/Mobile Number"). If not, visit an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or enrolment centre. Online account opening needs Aadhaar OTPs for e-KYC and e-sign; without a linked mobile you're pushed to the slow offline process.
  3. Do the names match? Compare PAN, Aadhaar and your bank account. Initials versus full names can fail the bank check, and the e-sign step needs your Aadhaar name to match. Fix any mismatch before you apply.
  4. Pick the bank account to link using the four criteria below.
  5. Gather what you'll type or photograph:
    • PAN number (and a photo of the card)
    • Bank account number and IFSC. A cancelled cheque or statement is only needed if the ₹1 bank check fails.
    • A clear signature on white paper (photo), or draw it on screen
    • Email, and a phone or laptop camera for the live photo
    • Nominee: name, relationship, date of birth
    • Occupation and an annual income range
  6. Optional: check your KYC status with your PAN on a KRA site such as CVL KRA (KYC inquiry). "KYC Validated" is best and "Registered" works. "On Hold" must be fixed with the broker before you can transact.

8 · Open the account (Zerodha, step by step) ⏱ 15–30 min ₹0 to open

Use the Kite app or signup.zerodha.com. Do it yourself, in one sitting, and never let an "agent" handle your OTPs.

  1. Enter your mobile number → Get OTP → enter it → Continue.
  2. Verify your email (choose Gmail, or type it) and enter the email OTP.
  3. Enter your PAN and date of birth.
  4. Choose segments: tick equity only. F&O and commodities need income proof, and you don't need them. Accept the terms.
  5. e-KYC through DigiLocker: enter Aadhaar, OTP, captcha and your 6-digit DigiLocker PIN. If you've never used DigiLocker, it asks you to set the PIN here.
  6. Personal details: parents' names, marital status, occupation, income range, and tax residency (India only; not a politically exposed person).
  7. Link your bank account by UPI (fastest) or by typing the account number and IFSC. Zerodha confirms it with a ₹1 "penny drop".
  8. In-person verification (IPV): allow camera access, face the camera in good light, and tap Capture.
  9. Signature: draw it on screen or upload your photo. It must match how you sign.
  10. Nominee: tap Add nominee and enter the details. Nominating someone (for example a parent) keeps assets from getting stuck. You can add or change it later.
  11. Sign now: e-sign the forms with an Aadhaar OTP. Your Aadhaar name must match, or the forms are rejected.
  12. Upload documents only if asked: bank proof if the penny drop failed, or income proof if you chose F&O (don't).
  13. Your user ID appears right after e-sign. Orders work only after the exchanges activate the account, usually within 48 working hours. Credentials arrive by email.
What happens while you wait (1–3 working days)Your KYC is validated at a KRA, a CDSL demat (BO ID) is created, and you're registered with NSE/BSE. If it takes longer than 3 working days, check your KRA status with your PAN and raise a ticket with the broker.
Common snags and fixes
  • Penny drop fails: upload a cancelled cheque or a statement showing your name, account number and IFSC.
  • DigiLocker PIN forgotten: reset it inside DigiLocker with an Aadhaar OTP, then return to the flow.
  • e-sign rejected for name mismatch: your Aadhaar name must match the account name; fix it at the source, then e-sign again.
  • No camera permission: enable it in the browser or app settings, or switch to the Kite app on your phone.
  • Aadhaar not linked to mobile: online opening isn't possible; use Zerodha's offline process or fix Aadhaar first (6).

9 · After activation: lock it down and get ready to sell ⏱ 30 min

  1. Log in to Kite (app and web), set a strong unique password, and turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, as Kite prompts you.
  2. Note your IDs in a password manager: the 6-character client ID, and the 16-digit demat ID (Console → Account → Demat, or console.zerodha.com/account/demat). You need the demat ID for net-banking ASBA.
  3. Set up selling authorisation now. Otherwise your first listing-day sell order fails at 10:00. Pick one:
    • CDSL TPIN Free CDSL emails or texts you a TPIN. On the selling day, pre-authorise your holdings after 07:00 (Holdings → authorise icon → Continue to CDSL → TPIN → OTP). It's valid for that day.
    • DDPI ₹100 + 18% GST = ₹118, once Console → Account → Demat → Enable DDPI → e-sign with an Aadhaar OTP. It's active within 24 working hours, and after that you sell without TPIN or OTP. Keep ₹118 in your Zerodha balance first.
    If you'll sell on listing days, DDPI removes a step at 9 AM, the most stressful minute. TPIN is fine if you practise it once.
  4. Add a small balance (₹200–500) to your Zerodha account by UPI for DDPI and charges. When paying a broker by UPI, the handle should contain @valid and your app shows a green triangle with a white thumbs-up.
  5. Register a backup UPI app on the same bank account: your bank's own app plus one of BHIM, PhonePe or Google Pay.
  6. Find net-banking ASBA in your bank's internet banking (usually "IPO" or "ASBA"). Note where it is and what it asks for: demat ID, depository CDSL, DP name Zerodha.
  7. Bookmark zerodha.com/ipo and learn where Kite keeps IPOs: Bids → IPO.

RuleDetail
UPI limitIndividual bids through brokers use UPI for up to ₹5 lakh, so your ₹15,000 is far inside it.
DeadlineThe mandate must be approved by 5 PM on the closing day. On Zerodha, orders placed after 4:45 PM are processed the next working day, and last-day orders after 3 PM go through only on a best-effort basis.
OwnershipThe UPI ID and bank account must be yours. Using someone else's gets the application rejected.
AppsCheck that your bank and UPI app are on NPCI's IPO live partners list. Users report CRED doesn't support IPO mandates, and UPI Lite can't be used.
Credit cardsCan't be used. ASBA blocks money in a bank account. And never borrow to apply.

If the mandate doesn't arrive

  1. Look in the UPI app under Mandates, Autopay or Pending requests. Notifications sometimes don't show.
  2. Check the UPI ID you typed, update the UPI app, and confirm your bank and app support IPO mandates.
  3. After about an hour with nothing: delete the application and re-submit (Zerodha's guidance), or use net-banking ASBA instead.
  4. Never keep two live bids. Cancel the first before re-bidding, or money can be blocked twice and both bids rejected.
  5. Kite's status can lag. What counts is the block in your bank: if the amount is blocked for a valid application, the registrar considers it for allotment.
Mind your EMIs and SIPsA block reduces your usable balance for about 5 working days, and unblocking sometimes runs late. Keep a cushion around auto-debit dates. If an unblock is delayed, SEBI's framework entitles you to compensation from the responsible bank (₹100 a day or 15% a year on the amount, whichever is higher). Complain to the bank in writing first, then on SCORES.

  • Use the ~2 PM figures on the closing day. Day 1 and day 2 are mostly retail noise. Institutions and big HNIs arrive on the final afternoon.

17 · Allotment day (T+1) and unblocking (T+2)

  1. When: the basis of allotment is published on the evening of T+1, sometimes late at night.
  2. Where: the registrar named in the RHP and on your broker's IPO page: KFin · MUFG Intime (formerly Link Intime) · Bigshare · Cameo · Maashitla. Or BSE's application status and NSE's "check IPO bids" page. Use the official sites, not third-party "checkers".
  3. How: choose the IPO, search by PAN (or application number or DP ID), solve the CAPTCHA. That's why no bot does this step for you.
  4. Allotted: on T+2 the amount is debited and the shares appear in your demat (CDSL sends an SMS or email). Kite may show the price as "NA" until listing. Now write your exit plan (18).

24 · Moving abroad later (NRI status)

  • Two different tests: under FEMA, leaving India for work or study with the intention to stay makes you non-resident. For income tax, your status depends on days spent in India. Tell your bank and broker promptly after you leave.
  • Bank: your resident savings account becomes an NRO account. Open an NRE account for foreign income you'll send home.
  • Demat and trading: you can keep your shares, but the resident demat is converted to an NRO (non-PIS) demat with an NRI trading account (brokers quote about 7–10 business days). Zerodha, Dhan, Upstox, ICICI Direct and Kotak support NRIs; HDFC Sky doesn't (HDFC uses a separate platform). That's one reason this guide recommends a single account.
  • IPOs as an NRI are allowed from an NRE or NRO account, not both. UPI on an international mobile number works only with some banks, so net-banking ASBA is the fallback.
  • Tax: India still taxes gains on Indian shares, and brokers deduct TDS for NRIs. The basic-exemption point above is for residents only. Canada taxes residents on worldwide income. Assets you bring are treated as bought at market value on arrival, Indian tax can usually be credited under the treaty, and form T1135 applies once foreign property costs more than CAD 100,000.
  • Mutual funds: many Indian fund houses restrict investors resident in Canada. A Nifty ETF in your demat converts more simply. Check with a cross-border adviser before you move.

"""IPO Radar Lite: one short Telegram message on the IPO days that matter.

What it does on each run
  1. Works out today's date in India (IST) and whether NSE is open.
  2. Reads watchlist.csv: the IPOs you (or Kiro) added, with dates and your label.
  3. Works out each IPO's allotment day (T+1), refund/credit day (T+2) and listing
     day (T+3), skipping weekends and NSE holidays, unless you typed the
     published dates yourself.
  4. Builds one message: closing today, listing today, allotment today, opening
     soon, open now, plus new IPO headlines from the ipowatch RSS feed.
  5. Sends it to your private Telegram chat, or prints it with --dry-run.

What it never does (see .kiro/steering/ipo-ground-rules.md)
  - Never places, changes or cancels a bid or an order. Never logs in anywhere.
  - Never asks for or stores PAN, UPI IDs, bank/demat/application numbers,
    passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or TPINs.
  - Reads one public RSS feed politely: robots.txt checked, descriptive
    User-Agent, at most one feed request per run, and it stops (no retry, no
    bypass) on HTTP 401/403/429 or a CAPTCHA page.

Standard library only; Python 3.9 or newer. Not investment advice.

Examples

class Calendar:
    """NSE trading days: weekdays that are not exchange holidays."""

    def __init__(self, holidays: dict[dt.date, str]):
        self.holidays = holidays
        self.years = {d.year for d in holidays}

    def is_trading_day(self, d: dt.date) -> bool:
        return d.weekday() < 5 and d not in self.holidays

    def next_trading_day(self, d: dt.date) -> dt.date:
        d += dt.timedelta(days=1)
        while not self.is_trading_day(d):
            d += dt.timedelta(days=1)
        return d

    def t_plus(self, t: dt.date, n: int) -> dt.date:
        """T+n in trading days, where T is the issue closing day."""
        d = t
        for _ in range(n):
            d = self.next_trading_day(d)
        return d

    def trading_days_ahead(self, d: dt.date, n: int) -> list[dt.date]:
        out, cur = [], d
        while len(out) < n:
            cur = self.next_trading_day(cur)
            out.append(cur)
        return out

    def covers(self, year: int) -> bool:
        return year in self.years


def load_holidays(path: Path | None) -> dict[dt.date, str]:
    holidays = {dt.date.fromisoformat(k): v for k, v in NSE_HOLIDAYS_2026.items()}
    if path and path.exists():
        for line in path.read_text(encoding="utf-8").splitlines():
            line = line.strip()
            if not line or line.startswith("#"):
                continue
            day, _, name = line.partition(" ")
            try:
                holidays[dt.date.fromisoformat(day)] = name.strip() or "NSE holiday"
            except ValueError:
                print(f"warning: ignoring bad line in {path.name}: {line!r}", file=sys.stderr)
    return holidays

@dataclasses.dataclass
class Ipo:
    name: str
    board: str
    open: dt.date
    close: dt.date
    allotment: dt.date
    listing: dt.date
    lot_size: int | None
    price_high: float | None
    registrar: str
    label: str
    score: str
    status: str
    exit_plan: str
    notes: str
    typed_dates: bool  # True when allotment/listing came from the file, not from T+n
    credit: dt.date | None = None  # T+2: refund (unblock) or share credit, set by load_watchlist

    @property
    def min_amount(self) -> int | None:
        if self.lot_size and self.price_high:
            return int(round(self.lot_size * self.price_high))
        return None


def parse_date(value: str, field: str, errors: list[str], row_no: int) -> dt.date | None:
    value = (value or "").strip()
    if not value:
        return None
    try:
        return dt.date.fromisoformat(value)
    except ValueError:
        errors.append(f"row {row_no}: '{field}' must be YYYY-MM-DD (got '{value}')")
        return None


def parse_number(value: str, field: str, errors: list[str], row_no: int, kind=float):
    value = (value or "").replace(",", "").replace("₹", "").strip()
    if not value:
        return None
    try:
        n = kind(float(value)) if kind is int else kind(value)
        if n <= 0:
            raise ValueError
        return n
    except ValueError:
        errors.append(f"row {row_no}: '{field}' must be a positive number (got '{value}')")
        return None


def load_watchlist(text: str, cal: Calendar) -> tuple[list[Ipo], list[str]]:
    """Parse watchlist CSV text. Returns (ipos, errors); bad rows are skipped, not fatal."""
    errors: list[str] = []
    ipos: list[Ipo] = []
    reader = csv.DictReader(io.StringIO(text.lstrip("\ufeff")))
    header = [h.strip().lower() for h in (reader.fieldnames or [])]
    missing = [c for c in REQUIRED if c not in header]
    if missing:
        return [], [f"header is missing column(s): {', '.join(missing)}"]
    reader.fieldnames = header
    for row_no, raw in enumerate(reader, start=2):  # row 1 is the header
        row = {k: (v or "").strip() for k, v in raw.items() if k}
        if not any(row.values()):
            continue
        if None in raw:  # more cells than headers: usually a comma inside a cell
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: too many cells (wrap text that contains commas in double quotes)")
            continue
        name = row.get("name", "")
        board = row.get("board", "").lower()
        label = row.get("label", "").lower()
        status = row.get("status", "").lower().replace(" ", "_").replace("-", "_")
        n_err = len(errors)
        if not name:
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'name' is empty")
        if board not in BOARDS:
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'board' must be mainboard or sme (got '{row.get('board', '')}')")
        if label not in LABELS:
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'label' must be apply, watch, skip or empty (got '{label}')")
        if status not in STATUSES:
            allowed = ", ".join(sorted(s for s in STATUSES if s))
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'status' must be one of {allowed} (got '{status}')")
        open_d = parse_date(row.get("open", ""), "open", errors, row_no)
        close_d = parse_date(row.get("close", ""), "close", errors, row_no)
        allot_d = parse_date(row.get("allotment", ""), "allotment", errors, row_no)
        list_d = parse_date(row.get("listing", ""), "listing", errors, row_no)
        lot = parse_number(row.get("lot_size", ""), "lot_size", errors, row_no, kind=int)
        price = parse_number(row.get("price_high", ""), "price_high", errors, row_no, kind=float)
        if open_d is None and not any("'open'" in e for e in errors[n_err:]):
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'open' date is required")
        if close_d is None and not any("'close'" in e for e in errors[n_err:]):
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'close' date is required")
        if open_d and close_d and close_d < open_d:
            errors.append(f"row {row_no}: 'close' ({close_d}) is before 'open' ({open_d})")
        if len(errors) > n_err:
            continue
        computed_allot = cal.t_plus(close_d, 1)
        computed_list = cal.t_plus(close_d, 3)
        ipo = Ipo(
            name=name, board=board, open=open_d, close=close_d,
            allotment=allot_d or computed_allot, listing=list_d or computed_list,
            lot_size=lot, price_high=price, registrar=row.get("registrar", ""),
            label=label, score=row.get("score", ""), status=status,
            exit_plan=row.get("exit_plan", ""), notes=row.get("notes", ""),
            typed_dates=bool(allot_d or list_d),
        )
        ipo.credit = cal.t_plus(close_d, 2)
        ipos.append(ipo)
    return ipos, errors

@dataclasses.dataclass
class Limits:
    mode: str = "paper"
    sme_allowed: bool = False
    max_per_ipo_inr: int = 15000
    max_blocked_inr: int = 30000
    max_applications_per_month: int = 6
    found: bool = False


def read_limits(path: Path) -> Limits:
    """Read the few keys the bot needs from risk-limits.yaml (no YAML library needed)."""
    lim = Limits()
    if not path.exists():
        return lim
    wanted = {"mode", "sme_allowed", "max_per_ipo_inr", "max_blocked_inr", "max_applications_per_month"}
    for line in path.read_text(encoding="utf-8").splitlines():
        m = re.match(r"^\s*([a-z_]+):\s*([^#\s]+)", line)
        if not m or m.group(1) not in wanted:
            continue
        key, value = m.group(1), m.group(2).strip("'\"")
        try:
            if key == "mode":
                lim.mode = value.lower()
            elif key == "sme_allowed":
                lim.sme_allowed = value.lower() == "true"
            else:
                setattr(lim, key, int(float(value)))
            lim.found = True
        except ValueError:
            pass
    return lim


def budget_line(ipos: list[Ipo], lim: Limits, today: dt.date) -> tuple[str, list[str]]:
    blocked = sum(i.min_amount or 0 for i in ipos if i.status == "applied")
    unknown = [i.name for i in ipos if i.status == "applied" and i.min_amount is None]
    used = sum(1 for i in ipos if i.status in APPLIED_STATUSES
               and (i.close.year, i.close.month) == (today.year, today.month))
    warnings = []
    if blocked > lim.max_blocked_inr:
        warnings.append(f"Blocked ₹{blocked:,} is over your ₹{lim.max_blocked_inr:,} limit.")
    if used > lim.max_applications_per_month:
        warnings.append(f"{used} applications this month is over your limit of {lim.max_applications_per_month}.")
    if unknown:
        warnings.append("Add lot_size and price_high for: " + ", ".join(unknown[:3]) + ".")
    line = (f"Mode {lim.mode} · cap ₹{lim.max_per_ipo_inr:,}/IPO · blocked ₹{blocked:,} of "
            f"₹{lim.max_blocked_inr:,} · {used}/{lim.max_applications_per_month} applications "
            f"this month · SME {'on' if lim.sme_allowed else 'off'}")
    if not lim.found:
        line += " (defaults: risk-limits.yaml not found)"
    return line, warnings

class Blocked(Exception):
    """The site refused us (401/403/429/CAPTCHA). Stop until tomorrow; never bypass."""


def polite_get(url: str, timeout: int = 20) -> bytes:
    host = urllib.parse.urlsplit(url).hostname or ""
    if host not in ALLOWED_HOSTS:
        raise ValueError(f"{host} is not on the allowlist")
    req = urllib.request.Request(url, headers={
        "User-Agent": USER_AGENT,
        "Accept": "application/rss+xml, application/xml;q=0.9, text/plain;q=0.8",
    })
    try:
        with urllib.request.urlopen(req, timeout=timeout) as resp:
            body = resp.read(3_000_000)
    except urllib.error.HTTPError as e:
        if e.code in (401, 403, 429):
            raise Blocked(f"HTTP {e.code}") from None
        raise
    head = body[:4000].lower()
    if b"captcha" in head or b"cf-chl" in head or b"challenge-platform" in head:
        raise Blocked("CAPTCHA or bot challenge")
    return body


def parse_feed(raw: bytes, since: dt.datetime, limit: int) -> list[tuple[str, str]]:
    root = ET.fromstring(raw)
    items = []
    for item in root.iter("item"):
        title = (item.findtext("title") or "").strip()
        link = (item.findtext("link") or "").strip()
        when = item.findtext("pubDate") or ""
        try:
            published = parsedate_to_datetime(when)
            if published.tzinfo is None:
                published = published.replace(tzinfo=dt.timezone.utc)
        except (TypeError, ValueError):
            continue
        if title and link.startswith("https://") and published >= since:
            items.append((title, link))
    return items[:limit]


def fetch_headlines(since: dt.datetime, limit: int) -> tuple[list[tuple[str, str]], str | None]:
    try:
        robots = polite_get(ROBOTS_URL).decode("utf-8", "replace")
        rp = urllib.robotparser.RobotFileParser()
        rp.parse(robots.splitlines())
        if not rp.can_fetch(USER_AGENT, FEED_URL):
            return [], "ipowatch robots.txt now disallows the feed, so it was skipped."
        time.sleep(2)  # be gentle between the two requests
        return parse_feed(polite_get(FEED_URL), since, limit), None
    except Blocked as e:
        return [], f"ipowatch refused the request ({e}); skipped until the next run, no bypass."
    except ET.ParseError:
        return [], "ipowatch feed format changed (parser drift); headlines skipped."
    except (urllib.error.URLError, TimeoutError, OSError, ValueError) as e:
        return [], f"ipowatch feed unavailable ({e.__class__.__name__}); headlines skipped."

def esc(s: str) -> str:
    return html.escape(s or "", quote=False)


def fmt_day(d: dt.date) -> str:
    return d.strftime("%a ") + str(d.day) + d.strftime(" %b")


def fmt_inr(n: float | int) -> str:
    return f"₹{n:,.0f}"


def label_text(i: Ipo) -> str:
    bits = []
    if i.label:
        bits.append(i.label.upper() + (f" {i.score}/20" if i.score else ""))
    if i.status:
        bits.append(i.status.replace("_", " "))
    return " · ".join(bits)


def registrar_links(i: Ipo) -> str:
    links = []
    key = i.registrar.lower()
    for k, (name, url) in REGISTRAR_LINKS.items():
        if k and k in key:
            links.append(f'<a href="{url}">{name}</a>')
            break
    links.append(f'<a href="{BSE_ALLOTMENT[1]}">{BSE_ALLOTMENT[0]}</a>')
    if not i.registrar:
        links.append("registrar named on your broker's IPO page")
    return " · ".join(links)


def ipo_line(i: Ipo, lim: Limits) -> str:
    line = f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b>"
    tag = label_text(i)
    if tag:
        line += f" · {esc(tag)}"
    if i.min_amount:
        line += f"\n  1 lot = {i.lot_size} × {fmt_inr(i.price_high)} = {fmt_inr(i.min_amount)}"
        if i.min_amount > lim.max_per_ipo_inr:
            line += " ⚠️ over your per-IPO cap"
    return line


def skip_by_rules(i: Ipo, lim: Limits) -> bool:
    return i.board == "sme" and not lim.sme_allowed


def is_paper(i: Ipo, lim: Limits) -> bool:
    return lim.mode != "live" or i.status in ("paper", "skipped")


def score_value(i: Ipo) -> float:
    try:
        return float(i.score)
    except ValueError:
        return 0.0


def by_label(ipos: list[Ipo]) -> list[Ipo]:
    order = {"apply": 0, "watch": 1, "skip": 2, "": 3}
    return sorted(ipos, key=lambda i: (order.get(i.label, 3), -score_value(i)))


def section(title: str, lines: list[str]) -> str:
    return f"<b>{title}</b>\n" + "\n".join(lines) if lines else ""


def fit(parts: list[str]) -> str:
    """Join message parts and keep the result under Telegram's limit."""
    text = "\n\n".join(p for p in parts if p)
    if len(text) <= MESSAGE_LIMIT:
        return text
    keep = []
    for p in parts:
        if p and len("\n\n".join(keep + [p])) <= MESSAGE_LIMIT - 80:
            keep.append(p)
    keep.append("<i>…trimmed to fit Telegram. Open watchlist.csv for the rest.</i>")
    return "\n\n".join(keep)


def header(title: str, now: dt.datetime) -> str:
    return f"<b>{esc(title)} · {fmt_day(now.date())} {now.year}, {now:%H:%M} IST</b>"


def warnings_block(errors: list[str], extra: list[str]) -> str:
    lines = [f"⚠️ watchlist.csv {esc(e)}" for e in errors[:3]]
    if len(errors) > 3:
        lines.append(f"⚠️ …and {len(errors) - 3} more watchlist problems.")
    lines += [f"⚠️ {esc(w)}" for w in extra]
    return "\n".join(lines)


def build_morning(now, ipos, errors, lim, cal, headlines, feed_note, coverage_note):
    today = now.date()
    sme_skipped = [i for i in ipos if skip_by_rules(i, lim) and i.open <= today <= i.listing]
    active = by_label([i for i in ipos if not skip_by_rules(i, lim)])

    closing = []
    for i in active:
        if i.close == today:
            todo = ("Paper: score it after ~14:00 with day-3 numbers and log APPLY / WATCH / SKIP."
                    if is_paper(i, lim) else
                    "Re-score after ~14:00 with day-3 numbers. Bid before 15:00; approve the UPI mandate by 17:00.")
            closing.append(ipo_line(i, lim) + "\n  " + todo)
    listing = []
    for i in active:
        if i.listing == today:
            plan = f" · your plan: {esc(i.exit_plan)}" if i.exit_plan else " · write your exit plan now"
            price = f" · issue {fmt_inr(i.price_high)}" if i.price_high else ""
            todo = ("Paper: write your guess before 09:00, note the 10:00 open and the close, log the result."
                    if is_paper(i, lim) else
                    "If allotted: authorise the sale with TPIN after 07:00 (skip if DDPI is on). Pre-open "
                    "09:00–09:45 takes limit orders only and can close any time after 09:35; trading from 10:00.")
            listing.append(f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b>{price}{plan}\n  {todo}")
    allot = [f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b> → check after ~18:00: {registrar_links(i)}"
             for i in active if i.allotment == today]
    credit = [f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b>: not allotted → money should be unblocked today; "
              "allotted → shares arrive in your demat." for i in active
              if i.credit == today and i.status in ("applied", "allotted", "not_allotted")]
    soon_days = set(cal.trading_days_ahead(today, 3))
    soon = [f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b> · opens {fmt_day(i.open)} · closes {fmt_day(i.close)}"
            + (f" · {esc(label_text(i))}" if label_text(i) else "") for i in active if i.open in soon_days]
    open_now = [f"• <b>{esc(i.name)}</b> · closes {fmt_day(i.close)}"
                + (f" · {esc(label_text(i))}" if label_text(i) else "")
                for i in active if i.open <= today < i.close]

    parts = [header("IPO Radar Lite", now)]
    warn = warnings_block(errors, [n for n in (coverage_note,) if n])
    if warn:
        parts.append(warn)
    body = [section("⏰ Closing today", closing), section("🔔 Listing today", listing),
            section("📬 Allotment today (T+1)", allot), section("💸 Refund / credit day (T+2)", credit),
            section("📅 Opening in the next 3 trading days", soon), section("📂 Open now", open_now)]
    body = [b for b in body if b]
    if not body:
        body = ["Nothing on your watchlist needs action today."]
    if sme_skipped:
        body.append(f"SME: {len(sme_skipped)} on your watchlist, skipped by your rules.")
    parts += body
    if headlines:
        parts.append(section("📰 New on ipowatch (24 h)",
                             [f'• <a href="{esc(u)}">{esc(t)}</a>' for t, u in headlines]))
    elif feed_note:
        parts.append(f"<i>{esc(feed_note)}</i>")
    line, budget_warn = budget_line(ipos, lim, today)
    if budget_warn:
        parts.append("\n".join(f"⚠️ {esc(w)}" for w in budget_warn))
    parts.append(f"<i>{esc(line)}</i>\n{FOOTER}")
    return fit(parts)


def build_closing(now, ipos, lim):
    today = now.date()
    closing = by_label([i for i in ipos if i.close == today and not skip_by_rules(i, lim)])
    if not closing:
        return None
    lines = [ipo_line(i, lim) for i in closing]
    if all(is_paper(i, lim) for i in closing):
        steps = ("Paper run: open the day-3 subscription by category (QIB / NII / retail) on two sources, "
                 "re-score factor G and log the label you would act on. No real bid.")
    else:
        steps = ("Now: open the day-3 subscription by category (QIB / NII / retail) on two sources and "
                 "re-score factor G. If it is still APPLY: broker app → IPO → Apply → 1 lot, cut-off, your "
                 "own UPI ID → approve the mandate in your UPI app straight away.\n"
                 "Zerodha sends last-day bids placed after 15:00 on a best-effort basis; bids close 16:45; "
                 "the mandate must be approved by 17:00. If no mandate arrives within an hour, delete and "
                 "re-submit (never keep two live bids).")
    line, _ = budget_line(ipos, lim, today)
    return fit([header("⏰ Closing today", now), "\n".join(lines), steps,
                f"<i>{esc(line)}</i>\n{FOOTER}"])


def build_weekly(now, ipos, errors, lim, cal, headlines, feed_note, coverage_note):
    today = now.date()
    days, cur = [], today
    while len(days) < 5:  # the next five weekdays (holidays included, so they can be shown)
        if cur.weekday() < 5:
            days.append(cur)
        cur += dt.timedelta(days=1)
    active = [i for i in ipos if not skip_by_rules(i, lim)]
    lines = []
    for d in days:
        if d in cal.holidays:
            lines.append(f"<b>{fmt_day(d)}</b>: NSE holiday ({esc(cal.holidays[d])})")
            continue
        events = []
        for i in active:
            for when, what in ((i.open, "opens"), (i.close, "closes"), (i.allotment, "allotment"),
                               (i.listing, "lists")):
                if when == d:
                    events.append(f"{esc(i.name)} {what}")
        if events:
            lines.append(f"<b>{fmt_day(d)}</b>: " + " · ".join(events))
    if not lines:
        lines = ["No watchlist events this week."]
    parts = [header(f"Week ahead {fmt_day(days[0])} – {fmt_day(days[-1])}", now)]
    warn = warnings_block(errors, [n for n in (coverage_note,) if n])
    if warn:
        parts.append(warn)
    parts.append("\n".join(lines))
    parts.append("<b>Tonight</b>: run /ipo-routine in Kiro (or review your Kiro automation's pull "
                 "request), update watchlist.csv, and set phone alarms for closing days (14:15) and "
                 "listing days (08:50).")
    if headlines:
        parts.append(section("📰 New on ipowatch (7 days)",
                             [f'• <a href="{esc(u)}">{esc(t)}</a>' for t, u in headlines]))
    elif feed_note:
        parts.append(f"<i>{esc(feed_note)}</i>")
    line, budget_warn = budget_line(ipos, lim, today)
    if budget_warn:
        parts.append("\n".join(f"⚠️ {esc(w)}" for w in budget_warn))
    parts.append(f"<i>{esc(line)}</i>\n{FOOTER}")
    return fit(parts)


def build_test(now, ipos, errors, lim, cal):
    nxt = cal.next_trading_day(now.date())
    return fit([header("✅ IPO Radar Lite is connected", now),

def today_context(now: dt.datetime, cal: Calendar) -> str:
    today = now.date()
    status = "open today" if cal.is_trading_day(today) else (
        f"closed today ({cal.holidays[today]})" if today in cal.holidays else "closed today (weekend)")
    nxt = cal.next_trading_day(today)
    upcoming = [f"{fmt_day(d)} {d.year} ({n})" for d, n in sorted(cal.holidays.items())
                if today < d <= today + dt.timedelta(days=60)]
    t = nxt if not cal.is_trading_day(today) else today
    return (f"IST now: {now:%a %d %b %Y %H:%M}. NSE: {status}; next trading day {fmt_day(nxt)} {nxt.year}. "
            f"An issue closing {fmt_day(t)} → allotment {fmt_day(cal.t_plus(t, 1))}, "
            f"credit/unblock {fmt_day(cal.t_plus(t, 2))}, listing {fmt_day(cal.t_plus(t, 3))}. "
            f"NSE holidays in the next 60 days: {', '.join(upcoming) if upcoming else 'none'}.")

def redact(text: str, secret: str) -> str:
    return text.replace(secret, "***") if secret else text


def strip_tags(text: str) -> str:
    return html.unescape(re.sub(r"<[^>]+>", "", text))


def send_telegram(text: str, token: str, chat_id: str, attempts: int = 3) -> bool:
    url = f"https://api.telegram.org/bot{token}/sendMessage"
    payload = {"chat_id": chat_id, "text": text, "parse_mode": "HTML", "disable_web_page_preview": "true"}
    error = "unknown error"
    for attempt in range(attempts):
        try:
            req = urllib.request.Request(url, data=urllib.parse.urlencode(payload).encode(),
                                         headers={"User-Agent": USER_AGENT})
            with urllib.request.urlopen(req, timeout=20) as resp:
                body = json.loads(resp.read().decode("utf-8"))
            if body.get("ok"):
                return True
            error = str(body.get("description"))
        except urllib.error.HTTPError as e:
            try:
                error = f"HTTP {e.code}: {json.loads(e.read().decode('utf-8')).get('description')}"
            except (ValueError, OSError):
                error = f"HTTP {e.code}"
            if e.code == 400 and "parse" in error.lower() and "parse_mode" in payload:
                payload = {"chat_id": chat_id, "text": strip_tags(text), "disable_web_page_preview": "true"}
                continue  # retry once as plain text
            if e.code in (400, 401, 403, 404):
                break  # wrong token, wrong chat ID or bot blocked: retrying will not help
        except (urllib.error.URLError, TimeoutError, OSError) as e:
            error = e.__class__.__name__
        time.sleep(2 ** (attempt + 1))
    print("Telegram send failed: " + redact(error, token), file=sys.stderr)
    return False

def find_chat_id(token: str) -> int:
    """Print the chat ID of whoever messaged the bot (you), so you can save TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID."""
    req = urllib.request.Request(f"https://api.telegram.org/bot{token}/getUpdates",
                                 headers={"User-Agent": USER_AGENT})
    try:
        with urllib.request.urlopen(req, timeout=20) as resp:
            body = json.loads(resp.read().decode("utf-8"))
    except urllib.error.HTTPError as e:
        print(f"Telegram answered HTTP {e.code}. Check the token (BotFather → /mybots → API Token).",
              file=sys.stderr)
        return 1
    except (urllib.error.URLError, TimeoutError, OSError) as e:
        print(f"Could not reach Telegram ({e.__class__.__name__}).", file=sys.stderr)
        return 1
    chats: dict[int, str] = {}
    for update in body.get("result", []):
        msg = update.get("message") or update.get("edited_message") or {}
        chat = msg.get("chat") or {}
        if "id" in chat:
            chats[chat["id"]] = chat.get("first_name") or chat.get("username") or chat.get("title") or ""
    if not chats:
        print("No messages yet. Open your bot in Telegram, tap Start (or send 'hi'), then run this again.")
        return 1
    for chat_id, name in chats.items():
        print(f"TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID={chat_id}    (chat with {name})")
    print("Save that number as the TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID secret.")
    return 0


def resolve_now(date_arg: str | None, time_arg: str | None, job: str) -> dt.datetime:
    if not date_arg and not time_arg:
        return dt.datetime.now(IST)
    day = dt.date.fromisoformat(date_arg) if date_arg else dt.datetime.now(IST).date()
    default = {"morning": "07:40", "closing": "14:15", "weekly": "18:10"}.get(job, "07:40")
    hh, mm = (time_arg or default).split(":")
    return dt.datetime(day.year, day.month, day.day, int(hh), int(mm), tzinfo=IST)


def pick_job(now: dt.datetime) -> str:
    if now.weekday() == 6:
        return "weekly"
    return "morning" if now.hour < 12 else "closing"


def coverage_warning(now: dt.datetime, cal: Calendar) -> str | None:
    horizon = now.date() + dt.timedelta(days=14)
    missing = sorted({y for y in (now.year, horizon.year) if not cal.covers(y)})
    if missing:
        return (f"NSE holidays for {', '.join(map(str, missing))} are missing, so T+n dates may be "
                "wrong. Add them to nse-holidays.txt.")
    return None

35 · FAQ

Can I use my parent's account or UPI to get a second chance?

No. Your bid must use your PAN, your demat and a UPI ID on your own bank account. A family member can apply for themselves with their own PAN and accounts, as their own decision. Operating their account for them isn't a workaround.

Do more lots, or the HNI category, improve my odds?

More retail lots: no; they only block more money. Small NII (₹2–10 lakh) is also a lottery, at its minimum ticket, which is far above your cap. Stay retail with 1 lot.

Should I pre-apply?

Not by default. It doesn't improve your odds, and your strategy decides on the closing day after 2 PM. Use it only if you've already decided and will be unreachable on the closing day.

Can I cancel after applying?

Yes, while the window is open (on Zerodha, 10:00–16:45 on trading days). The block is then released. Never place a second bid before cancelling the first.

Is GMP legal? Should I trust it?

GMP is an unofficial, unregulated quote. Looking at it is harmless, but it's noisy, so it gets at most 1 point. Trading in the grey market ("kostak", "subject-to") gives you no protection. Never do it.

I got allotted in a weak IPO and it opened below issue. Now what?

Exit that day, as your plan says. The listing-gain thesis failed. Don't average down, and don't "wait for it to come back". The only exception is a score ≥ 16 plus a 12-month thesis you wrote before listing.

Can I keep a good IPO for the long term?

Only if you'd happily buy it at the listing price and hold it for 12+ months. That's a different decision from flipping. After 12 months, gains are long-term (12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh a year).

Does my employer care?

Check your employer's code of conduct and insider-trading policy. Many restrict or pre-clear trading in the employer's own shares (and sometimes clients' shares), and some require disclosure. General IPO applications are usually fine for most employees, but confirm it for your role.

I'll be busy at 2 PM on a closing day. What do I do?

Skip it, or decide in the morning using the day-2 numbers and accept that the information is weaker. Never rush a bid between meetings. If a closing-day check from Kiro (L2) arrives at 14:15, it can save you the research, but the bid is still yours.

Can the bot apply for me?

No, by design: you approve every UPI mandate yourself, broker terms forbid unofficial bots, and a wrong automated bid can lock real money. The automation does the research, the reminders and the record-keeping.

Is my IPO profit tax-free if my income is under ₹12 lakh?

No. Short-term gains are taxed at a special rate (20% + 4% cess) and the s.87A rebate doesn't cover them. But if your taxable salary is under the ₹4 lakh basic exemption, the unused part absorbs STCG first.

How do I know a UPI ID really belongs to my broker?

Brokers' collection handles contain @valid, and your UPI app shows a green triangle with a white thumbs-up. You can verify any UPI ID or bank account in SEBI Check before paying.
