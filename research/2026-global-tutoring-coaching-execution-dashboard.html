Four-week teaching path

Before lesson 1 — diagnose
  • Map the current process, frequency, time, defects, and desired result.
  • Classify data and approve only safe examples.
  • Agree on one baseline metric and written scope.
  • Confirm payment, accessibility needs, cancellation terms, and recording choice.
Week 1 — map
  • Map trigger, inputs, decisions, output, reviewer, and failure cost.
  • Select public, synthetic, redacted, or explicitly approved data.
  • Run the existing process once and record friction.
Week 2 — build
  • Teach decomposition, context, examples, and required output format.
  • Build the smallest reusable instruction set.
  • Add a verification checklist and escalation rule.
Week 3 — test
  • Run multiple test cases and score output against the checklist.
  • Find recurring errors and weak inputs.
  • Create version 2 plus a manual fallback.
Week 4 — hand over
  • Observe the client running the workflow.
  • Finalize SOP, data rules, quality checks, and 30-day practice plan.
  • Compare baseline and end measurement without claiming causality you cannot prove.

Session SOP

24 hours before
  • Confirm payment or funded milestone.
  • Confirm agenda, homework, approved data, and accessibility needs.
  • Test meeting link, screen share, audio, source files, and backup internet.
  • Check that no sensitive file was sent accidentally; delete or return it safely.
During the session
  • Restate the objective, boundary, and baseline.
  • Teach one concept, demonstrate once, then let the client drive.
  • The client operates the workflow for at least half the session.
  • Score output against the checklist; record uncertainty and next action.
  • Do not accept passwords or enter a production system.
Within 24 hours after
  • Send a five-bullet recap, approved template version, homework, due date, and risks.
  • Log live, prep, review, and support time separately.
  • Confirm the next meeting and any data the client must redact.
  • Delete temporary data according to the agreed retention date.
After the final handover
  • Observe a final client-operated run.
  • Deliver SOP, fallback, quality checklist, and 30-day plan.
  • Request feedback without requiring positive wording.
  • Request testimonial, case study, or referral only through separate optional consent.

Capacity limits

  • Maximum 6 concurrent 1:1 clients until delivery is stable.
  • Maximum 3 live calls/day.
  • No more than 2 consecutive late-evening days.
  • First-six-client week: 10 live + 8 prep/review + 10 sales + 4 admin + 3 learning = 35 hours.
  • Raise price or add a gated cohort before adding unsustainable hours.
