20-second in-person/warm opener

Hi, I’m [name], based nearby. I turn one completed renovation into a truthful project-proof set—12 photos, three short videos, one Google update, and a trackable enquiry path. I’m not selling ads or promising leads. Before I show anything, may I ask three questions to see if you already solve this in-house?

Three qualification questions

  1. How many residential interior projects did you complete in the last 12 months, and is one handover-ready in the next 14 days?
  2. Show me the last completed project you captured, published, and tied to an enquiry source.
  3. Who owns approvals and enquiry follow-up, and can they update a simple status log weekly?

    What evidence does—and does not—say

    • Google confirms free business media, updates, interactions, and owner controls.
    • Meta reports messaging is mainstream in India.
    • Udyam and local-service data establish a large business context.
    • None proves contractors buy this service.
    • None proves an update creates an enquiry.
    • Paid deposits, renewals, attributable qualified enquiries, and contribution/hour are the deciding evidence.
    Central uncertainty: route-specific Indian buyer-demand data is absent. That is why Days 14, 30, 60, and 90 are kill gates.
